Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

WhisperBook collects no personal data.

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your books, settings and reading progress stay on your device. The developer runs no servers and receives nothing from the app.

Data stored on your device

To work, WhisperBook keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Books and documents you import (EPUB, PDF, FB2) and books or papers you download from the catalog
  • Reading settings: font, size, spacing, theme and colors, voice, speed and pitch
  • Reading positions, bookmarks and the sleep timer setting
  • The number of free imports used this month, whether Pro is unlocked and when a free trial ends

Book files and their extracted content are excluded from iCloud backup to save your iCloud storage. Settings may be included in your normal device backup, which Apple handles.

Speech

Books are read aloud with the text-to-speech voices built into iOS and iPadOS. Speech is generated on your device; the text is not sent anywhere.

Home Screen widget

The Continue Listening widget shows the book you last opened. The app shares its title, author, current chapter and a small cover image with the widget through an App Group container on your device. Nothing leaves the device.

Read Aloud from Safari and other apps

When you choose “WhisperBook Read Aloud” in the share sheet, the extension receives the page title, address and text, or the text you selected. It reads that text aloud on your device and discards it when you close it. It is not saved or sent anywhere.

Free catalogs: Project Gutenberg and arXiv

Browsing, searching and downloading in the in-app catalogs connects directly to these public services:

  • Project Gutenberg: the book list comes from Gutendex (gutendex.com), a volunteer-run index of Project Gutenberg; books and cover images come from gutenberg.org. See Project Gutenberg's privacy policy.
  • arXiv: paper lists come from the arXiv API (export.arxiv.org); papers download from arxiv.org. See arXiv's privacy policy.

Like any website you visit, these services can see your IP address and the search words you type into the catalog. WhisperBook sends no account, device identifier or other personal information with these requests. If you never open the catalog, the app makes no network requests apart from App Store purchases.

Translation

If you select text in a book and choose Translate, the text is handled by Apple's built-in translation feature under Apple's privacy policy. WhisperBook does not receive or store it.

In-app purchases and the free trial

WhisperBook Pro, the Supporter purchase and the free 3-day trial are handled entirely by the App Store. WhisperBook never sees your payment details or Apple Account. See Apple's privacy policy.

No analytics or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking, no tracking domains and no collected data types. The only third-party code is ZIPFoundation, an open-source library that unpacks EPUB and zipped FB2 files on the device and makes no network requests.

Children

WhisperBook collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. WhisperBook has no way to contact you directly because it does not collect contact details.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the WhisperBook support page on GitHub.
